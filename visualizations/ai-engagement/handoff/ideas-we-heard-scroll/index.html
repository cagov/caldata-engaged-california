Ideas we heard

Our poll asked people what they’d like government to do about the impact of AI. We got a wide range of ideas for policies.

Our poll asked what you’d like government to do about the impact of AI. It was open to all Californians.

We got a wide range of ideas for good policies.

Then we refined those ideas in 2-hour discussions,

and grouped what people said in these discussions into 27 distinct ideas.

 

Word size = votes received in the discussions

Then we ranked the 27 ideas by how often they came up and how much support they drew. About a third of the single-mention poll ideas are hidden here to keep the cloud legible — every idea that reached the 27-idea shortlist is shown in full.

Self-selected public consultation, not a probability sample. Percentages and counts describe the people who responded, not Californians as a whole.
