Phase 1

In May 2026, we opened a public forum on the Impact of AI on work.

Over many months, a cross-section of the public came together to give us their thoughts. They took polls and joined facilitated group discussions.

From phase 1 to phase 2

They shared their own experiences, and listened to their peers.

Then they refined gut reactions into concrete policy ideas.

Phase 2

One idea came up consistently: We need humans in the AI policy loop.

Not only in how AI tools are used day-to-day, but in how they are built and governed.
