Ideas we heard

Our poll asked what you’d like government to do about the impact of AI. We got a wide range of ideas for good policies.

Then we refined those ideas in 2-hour discussions. We grouped what people said in these discussions into 27 distinct ideas. Then we ranked them according to:

  • How often an idea came up across discussions (frequency), and
  • How much support it drew when it did (how often it was a popular idea).
