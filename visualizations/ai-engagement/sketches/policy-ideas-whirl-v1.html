Prototype sketch — Phase 1 → Phase 2

From a wide range of ideas to 27 shared ones

Scroll to see how thousands of open-ended survey answers narrowed into the policy ideas Californians discussed and voted on.

Our poll asked what you’d like government to do about the impact of AI. It was open to all Californians.

We got a wide range of ideas for good policies.

Then we refined those ideas in 2-hour discussions,

and grouped what people said in these discussions into 27 distinct ideas.

Prototype on published data. The Phase 1 ideas are the 229 poll labels published on engaged.ca.gov (/public/data/ai-report-ideas-cloud.json), with their published counts and subthemes. An earlier internal version of this sketch used a larger, unpublished label set; it is not in this repo.
The two sets are linked by subtheme only. There is no id connecting any of the 337 Phase 1 ideas to any of the 27 Phase 2 ideas — only the 8 subthemes are shared between the two exports. The animation above never claims a specific Phase 1 idea became a specific Phase 2 idea; it shows the 8 subthemes consolidating, then the 27 ideas arriving separately.
Self-selected public consultation, not a probability sample. Percentages and counts describe the people who responded, not Californians as a whole.
